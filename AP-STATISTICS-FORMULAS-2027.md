## Current exam context

The revised AP Statistics course is effective for 2026–27. The May 2027 exam is fully digital, with 42 multiple-choice questions and 4 free-response questions. Reference information is available in Bluebook and in printed form. The current course is organized into five units and no longer includes several topics from the older framework, including geometric distributions, combining random variables, chi-square goodness-of-fit, and inference for a population regression slope.

## Recommended workflow

1. Identify the statistical target.
2. Classify the response variable.
3. Identify whether the design is one-sample, two-sample, paired, tabular, or regression-based.
4. Decide whether the goal is description, estimation, testing, association, or prediction.
5. Check the relevant conditions.
6. Select the formula or procedure.
7. Calculate.
8. Interpret the result in context and within the scope supported by the design.
