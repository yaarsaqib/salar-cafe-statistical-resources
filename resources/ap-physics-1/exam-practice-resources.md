## Laboratory and experimental-design preparation

- [AP Physics 1 Labs and Experimental Investigations](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/labs/) — experimental planning, variables, measurements, graph choice, uncertainty, evidence, and laboratory-report reasoning.

Laboratory work matters because AP Physics 1 assesses experimental design and data-analysis skills directly. Students should be able to identify independent, dependent, and controlled variables; select suitable equipment; plan repeatable measurements; choose a graph that tests the proposed relationship; and discuss uncertainty and limitations.

## Recommended workflow

1. Learn the concept through the relevant [unit and topic lesson](README.md).
2. Use the [formula sheet](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/formula-sheet/) as a relationship map, not a substitution list.
3. Complete topic-focused [MCQ practice](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/practice-test/mcq-practice/) and [FRQ practice](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/practice-test/frq-practice/).
4. Practice experimental reasoning with the [labs guide](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/labs/).
5. Move to the [five full model papers](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/practice-test/) under timed conditions.
6. Use the [exam-format](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/exam-format/) and [exam-length](https://onlineinternetcafe.com/ap-physics-1/exam-length/) resources to refine pacing.
