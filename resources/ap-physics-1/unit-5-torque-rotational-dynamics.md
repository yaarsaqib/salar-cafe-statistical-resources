# AP Physics 1 Unit 5 — Torque and Rotational Dynamics

Unit 5 extends linear kinematics and Newton's laws to rotating rigid systems. It connects angular variables, torque, rotational inertia, equilibrium, and rotational acceleration.

## Study checkpoint

Always state the rotation axis before calculating torque or rotational inertia. A force can be large and still produce little or no torque if its line of action passes close to the axis.
