# AP Physics 1 Unit 1 — Kinematics

Kinematics describes motion before asking what force caused it. The unit builds the language used everywhere else in AP Physics 1: vectors, displacement, velocity, acceleration, graphs, reference frames, and two-dimensional motion.

## Study checkpoint

A useful Unit 1 check is whether you can look at a motion graph and state the sign of velocity and acceleration without treating the graph as a picture of the physical path. You should also be able to choose a positive axis, maintain that sign convention, and separate x and y components in projectile motion.
