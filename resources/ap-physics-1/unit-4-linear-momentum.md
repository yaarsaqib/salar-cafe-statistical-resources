# AP Physics 1 Unit 4 — Linear Momentum

Unit 4 describes interactions through momentum and impulse, especially short-duration forces, recoil, and collisions. The central habit is to choose a system and decide whether external impulse is negligible during the interval of interest.

## Study checkpoint

Momentum conservation is a statement about a system, not automatically about each object. In a collision, identify the system, define the interval, and check whether external impulse is negligible before writing a conservation equation.
