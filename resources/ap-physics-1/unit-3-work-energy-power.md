# AP Physics 1 Unit 3 — Work, Energy, and Power

Unit 3 uses energy accounting to predict and explain motion. It connects translational kinetic energy, work, potential energy, conservation of energy, and power.

## Study checkpoint

Energy methods are especially useful when the detailed time history of the force is not needed. Define the system first, distinguish stored energy from transfers, and check whether nonconservative transfers such as friction must be included.
