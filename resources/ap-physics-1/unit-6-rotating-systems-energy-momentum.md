# AP Physics 1 Unit 6 — Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems

Unit 6 applies energy and angular-momentum ideas to rotating, rolling, and orbiting systems. It links rotational kinetic energy, torque-work, angular impulse, angular momentum conservation, rolling, and satellite motion.

## Study checkpoint

For a rolling object, keep translational and rotational energy terms distinct. For angular momentum, specify both the system and the axis before deciding whether conservation applies.
