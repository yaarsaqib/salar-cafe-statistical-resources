## How to use this map

1. Begin with the complete AP Physics 1 hub to understand the exam structure and the role of each unit.
2. Open the unit map matching the concept you are studying.
3. Use the topic-level links to reach the full explanation, equations, worked reasoning, misconceptions, and practice for that topic.
4. Return to mixed practice only after the individual concepts and representations are secure.

## Representation-first study principle

AP Physics 1 is not only about substituting numbers into equations. The same physical situation may be expressed as a diagram, graph, equation, experiment, or written explanation. A strong study sequence therefore asks four questions repeatedly:

- What system or object is being analyzed?
- Which physical principle applies?
- Which representation makes the relationship clearest?
- Does the final result agree with the direction, units, graph shape, and limiting behavior?
