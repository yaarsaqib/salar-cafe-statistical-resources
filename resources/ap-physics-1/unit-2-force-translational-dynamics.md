# AP Physics 1 Unit 2 — Force and Translational Dynamics

Unit 2 explains how interactions change the translational motion of a system. It connects system boundaries, center of mass, free-body diagrams, Newton's laws, gravity, friction, springs, and circular motion.

## Study checkpoint

Before calculating, identify the system and every external interaction. A free-body diagram should contain forces, not velocity arrows or invented 'motion forces.' For circular motion, remember that 'centripetal' describes the inward net-force requirement rather than a new force that must be added to the diagram.
